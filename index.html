About FraudLens
What it does

FraudLens is an AI-powered transaction risk scoring tool for fintech and banking teams. Upload a CSV of financial transactions and receive instant risk analysis — every transaction is scored 0–100, categorised as High / Medium / Low risk, and flagged with specific fraud signals such as velocity attacks, geographic anomalies, unusual timing, and merchant risk patterns.

Who it's for

Fraud analysts, compliance officers, fintech product managers, and risk teams at banks, NBFCs, payment gateways, and digital lending platforms who need a fast first-pass risk triage on transaction data without setting up complex infrastructure.

Technology stack

Google Gemini 2.5 Flash (AI engine) · Direct browser API calls (zero backend) · Vanilla HTML / CSS / JS · localStorage for key and preferences · Zero backend infrastructure · Cost to run: ₹0

Fraud signals detected

Velocity attacks · Geographic anomalies · Cross-border irregularities · Amount spikes · Merchant risk · Card-not-present fraud · Unusual transaction timing · Duplicate merchant patterns
